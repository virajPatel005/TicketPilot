## Symptoms

Your account is disabled after several wrong passwords, for example after
signing in on a new device or after a password synchronisation problem. The
message may say the account is locked or that sign-in is blocked. Waiting
usually does not unlock it immediately, and repeated attempts can extend
the lock.

## Steps to fix

1. Stop trying to sign in for 30 minutes so the lock can clear or expire.
2. Check for anything still signing in with the old password, such as a
   phone, tablet, or a mapped drive, and update or disconnect it.
3. Use "Forgot password" to set a new password, then sign in only once to
   test it.
4. Reconnect other apps one at a time with the new password.
5. If a service account or shared workstation is involved, note the device
   name and include it in your ticket.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the Service Desk if the account is still locked after 30
minutes, if a new password does not help, or if the lock keeps returning.
Repeated locks, or locks on an account you do not recognise, should be
reported to the Security Team as a possible attack.
