## Symptoms

An application opens but shows an expired, missing, or invalid licence
message, or it stops working after a renewal date. You may have been moved
to a new team, or the licence pool for your department may be full. Some
tools show the licence error only when you try to save or export.

## Steps to fix

1. Note the exact message, the application name, and the date it started.
2. Sign out of the application and sign back in with your work account.
3. Check the licence page in the app to see which licence is assigned to
   you and when it expires.
4. If you changed teams or roles recently, mention this in your ticket,
   since your licence may be tied to your old team.
5. Keep working in a web or read-only version if available while you wait.

## When to escalate

Escalate to IT Support if the licence is expired, missing, or assigned to
someone else, or if a deadline depends on the tool. Do not share licence
keys or use another person's account to work around the problem.
