## Symptoms

You cannot join the office Wi-Fi, the connection drops every few minutes, or
you can connect but have no internet access. The signal may show as weak,
or the network may ask for a password you do not have. Problems can be
worse in particular rooms or on particular floors.

## Steps to fix

1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on, forget the office network, and reconnect.
2. Move closer to the nearest access point to rule out a coverage gap.
3. Restart your device, then try a second device to see if the fault is
   specific to your device.
4. Check that your device is not in airplane mode and that VPN is off
   while you test the raw Wi-Fi connection.
5. Record your room, the network name shown, and the exact error before
   you submit a ticket.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the Network Team if reconnection fails, if the fault affects a
whole floor or many users, or if it recurs after a restart. Do not use a
personal hotspot for trial data; report the outage instead so it can be
fixed properly.
