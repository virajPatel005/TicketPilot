## Symptoms

You cannot sign in because your password is rejected, or you have forgotten
it completely. The login page may say the credentials are invalid, or may
force a password change at next sign-in. This is different from an account
lockout, where the account is disabled after repeated failed attempts.

## Steps to fix

1. On the sign-in page, choose "Forgot password" and enter your work email.
2. Open the reset email, open the link within 60 minutes, and set a new
   password of at least 12 characters with mixed case and a number.
3. Sign in on one device first to confirm the new password works.
4. Update saved passwords on other devices and apps, such as email and VPN.
5. If the reset link expires, request a new one instead of reusing the old
   link, and check your spam folder if the email does not arrive.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the Service Desk if no reset email arrives after 15 minutes, if
the reset page loops back to sign-in, or if your account shows as locked.
Do not share your password or reset link with anyone, including help staff.
