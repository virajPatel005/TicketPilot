## Symptoms

Your laptop takes a long time to start, freezes, or slows to a crawl when
you open large files or several applications. The fan may run constantly,
the battery may drain quickly, or Windows may report the disk or memory as
nearly full. The slowdown may happen only at certain times of day.

## Steps to fix

1. Restart the laptop fully, not just sleep, and close unnecessary apps.
2. Check free disk space and delete or archive large personal files to an
   approved location.
3. Open Task Manager and note any program using most of the CPU, memory,
   or disk at the time of the slowdown.
4. Run the standard Windows update check and restart if updates are pending.
5. Note when the problem started and what you were doing, then submit a
   ticket with those details and your device name.

## When to escalate

Escalate to IT Support if the laptop is unusable, if it fails to start, or
if it keeps overheating or shutting down. Back up important files first if
you still can, and do not open the device yourself.
