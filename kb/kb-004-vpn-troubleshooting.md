## Symptoms

The VPN client fails to connect, connects then immediately drops, or lets
you connect but then blocks internal sites. You may see a timeout, an
authentication error, or a message that the tunnel could not be established.
The problem may affect one device, one network, or the whole office.

## Steps to fix

1. Disconnect the VPN, wait one minute, and reconnect using the standard
   profile rather than a custom one.
2. Restart the VPN client, then restart your device if the error persists.
3. Try a different network, such as your phone hotspot, to separate a local
   network problem from a VPN problem.
4. Confirm you are using the current client version and that your password
   and MFA are up to date.
5. Note the exact error message, the time it happened, and whether other
   people are affected, then include these details in your ticket.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the Network Team if the VPN still fails after these steps, if
many users are affected, or if internal trial systems are unreachable.
Repeated disconnects during urgent work should be marked high priority.
