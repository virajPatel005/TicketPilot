## Symptoms

Your laptop, phone, or tablet is missing, stolen, or left somewhere you
cannot return to. The device may contain work email, trial documents, or
saved passwords. You may still be signed in on it, and remote wipe may be
needed before anyone else can access it.

## Steps to fix

1. Report the loss immediately through the lost-device process or ticket,
   including the device type and asset tag if known.
2. Change your password and review MFA settings from another device.
3. If remote locate or wipe is available, check whether the device can be
   found or locked, but do not travel to unsafe locations to retrieve it.
4. Note when and where you last had the device, and who may have seen it.
5. Request a replacement or loan device through the normal process.

## When to escalate

Escalate to IT Support and the Security Team at once for theft, for any
device holding trial data, or if the device cannot be wiped. Speed
matters: reporting within the first hour gives the best chance of
protecting the data.
