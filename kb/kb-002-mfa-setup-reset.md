## Symptoms

You are asked for a verification code but cannot complete sign-in. Common
causes are a new phone, a restored phone, an authenticator app that was
reinstalled, or a time mismatch that makes codes invalid. You may be locked
out of every app that requires multi-factor authentication (MFA).

## Steps to fix

1. Use the backup codes you saved when MFA was first set up, if you have them.
2. If you still have your old device, open the authenticator app and add the
   new device from the account settings before removing the old one.
3. Check that your phone date and time are set to automatic, then retry.
4. If the authenticator app was reinstalled, sign in to your account page
   with a backup code and set up the authenticator again from scratch.
5. Save the new backup codes somewhere safe and offline, not in a screenshot.

## When to escalate

Escalate to IT Support if you have no backup codes and no old device. You
will need to verify your identity, for example with your manager, before
MFA is reset. Never accept a reset from someone who contacts you by phone
or email without you asking first.
