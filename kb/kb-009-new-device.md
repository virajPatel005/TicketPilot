## Symptoms

You need a laptop or desktop for a new role, a replacement for a broken
device, or a second device for field work. You may not know which standard
models are available, or your old device may be too old to support the
software you need for your role.

## Steps to fix

1. Ask your manager to confirm the business need, since device requests
   require local approval.
2. Submit a request describing your role, the software you must run, and
   whether you need a laptop, dock, or accessories.
3. If you are replacing a device, note its asset tag and any faults, and
   back up your files to the approved storage.
4. Wait for the standard build and delivery notice before assuming a date.
5. On delivery, sign in, check your apps, and return the old device with
   its charger as instructed.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the Service Desk if a device is broken and you cannot work, if
a delivery is overdue, or if the device arrived damaged. Urgent clinical
trial work should be marked with the deadline in your request.
