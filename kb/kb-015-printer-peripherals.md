## Symptoms

A printer shows as offline, prints blank or garbled pages, or jobs stay in
the queue. A mouse, keyboard, headset, or monitor may not be detected, or
may work only when plugged into a particular port. The problem may affect
one printer or every printer on your floor.

## Steps to fix

1. Restart the printer and your device, and check that cables and power
   are firmly connected.
2. Remove stuck jobs from the print queue and print one test page.
3. For peripherals, try a different USB port and, if possible, a second
   device to separate a device fault from a port fault.
4. Check the printer's own display for error messages and note them.
5. Submit a ticket with the printer name, location, and error message.

## When to escalate

Escalate to IT Support if the printer is offline for everyone, if paper
or toner is jammed or missing, or if a peripheral sparks, smells of
burning, or repeatedly disconnects. Do not open printers or devices to
fix hardware faults yourself.
