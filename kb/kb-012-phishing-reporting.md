## Symptoms

You received a suspicious email, message, or call asking for your password,
an MFA code, payment, or personal details, often with urgent or threatening
language. The sender address may look slightly wrong, links may point to
unfamiliar sites, or attachments may be unexpected.

## Steps to fix

1. Do not click links, open attachments, reply, or enter any details.
2. Report the message using the phishing report button in your mail app,
   or forward it to the Security Team address.
3. If you already clicked or entered details, change your password at
   once, review your MFA settings, and tell the Security Team immediately.
4. Delete the message after reporting, and check your sent folder for
   anything you may have sent in error.
5. Warn colleagues only if they may have received the same message.

## When to escalate

Escalate to the Security Team at once if you clicked, if money or data was
sent, or if the message targets clinical trial systems. When in doubt,
treat it as real and report it; the Security Team would rather check a
harmless message than miss an attack.
